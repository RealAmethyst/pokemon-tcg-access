## Features
- the number keys now jump straight to a top-bar screen: 1 Home, 2 Decks, 3 Profile, 4 Shop, 5 Battle Pass, 6 Card Dex — from any HUB screen, without arrowing across the bar first. Works on the number row or the numpad. Pressing the number of the screen you're already on says its name; a number does nothing when the bar isn't there (in a battle), while a popup is covering it, or while you're typing in a text box
- the season reward popup is supported — the one that welcomes you to a new ladder after the tutorial and at every season rollover. It reads the season, your new league, your trophies and the reward cards, and you can collect them. Previously it sat on screen silently while the keyboard stayed on the screen behind it

## Bug fixes
- fixed being unable to activate a Stadium card's ability — the Surfing Beach stadium (and any stadium whose ability opens a follow-up choice) read as "unavailable" even though the tutorial was pointing right at it. When the tutorial spotlights a card this way it never marks one specific action, and the mod was mistaking that for "the tutorial hasn't unlocked this yet" and greying the ability out. It now trusts the game's own on/off state for the button, so the ability activates. The guard that stops you firing an attack before a tutorial attack step is ready is unchanged
- moving damage counters now works on the board like a sighted player does it — you pick which Pokémon to move damage from, then drop counters onto the target — instead of a behind-the-scenes shortcut. This fixes the tutorial freezing on the "move damage counters" step (Munkidori's ability and the like): the tutorial watches you pick the damage up and drop it, and the old shortcut did neither, so it hung
- you can now move an energy between your Pokémon — Mega Venusaur ex's Solar Transfer and any effect like it. Go to a Pokémon that has an energy to move (it says so as you arrow across the board) and press enter to lift one off, then go to the Pokémon you want it on (it says "can receive energy") and press enter to drop it. Backspace puts it back if you grab the wrong one. Before this, the move opened but there was no way to pick anything up
- fixed the Hilda step in the tutorial where you couldn't choose the Pokémon and energy to put into your hand — nothing happened when you pressed enter on a card. The mod was covering the professor's message so you could never advance it, and the game won't unlock the cards until you do; the selection now waits underneath the message so you read it, move past it, then pick your cards
- the deck editor's "exchange all unowned" option now appears as soon as the game shows it, instead of only after you opened and closed something else. The game hides that button until it has checked your credit balance, so every list we built before that check finished was missing it
- fixed the tutorial getting stuck on the shop step: the tab it asked you to open ignored enter and said nothing. Any tutorial step that points at a tab now works with enter, on every screen
- fixed the whole match freezing when an attack asks you to choose another attack to use — Slowking's Seek Inspiration, Zoroark's Foul Play and the like. The mod crashed while reading the popup, and the crash took the game's match handling down with it. That kind of crash can no longer escape the mod
- you can now choose an attack in that popup: each option reads its name, damage and what it does, and enter uses it
- the mod no longer reads out parts of a popup that are hidden on screen
- fixed the deck editor tutorial removing a card when it asked you to add one — if your deck already held that card, the mod jumped to the deck's copy, and the tutorial accepted the wrong edit and moved on
- fixed the tutorial losing control of the keyboard after a message closed — you could wander into screens the tutorial had locked, and its next message would speak without you being able to answer it
- fixed the game locking up after collecting the tutorial's battle pass reward — a dialogue that closed behind the reward popup kept hold of the keyboard forever (the same fault is now impossible on every screen)
- COLLECT ALL now says "unavailable" during the tutorial's card reveal instead of silently doing nothing — the tutorial disables it so you reveal each card yourself
- fixed the tutorial's reward popup putting your focus on the wrong button — it now takes you straight to COLLECT REWARDS, the only button the tutorial lets you press
- fixed being unable to pick more cards from a search pile if you chose the first one the instant the pile opened — the cards are now selected once they've finished moving, and your keypress is held rather than dropped
- fixed the tutorial freezing the whole match if you attacked the instant the attack menu opened — the attack is now held until the tutorial has actually asked for it
- fixed being able to press Done on a tutorial's attach step without attaching anything, which left the tutorial waiting forever
- fixed the tutorial refusing to let you pick an attack when it was asking you to — you no longer have to close the attack menu and reopen it
- fixed being able to retreat during a tutorial step that didn't allow it, which could leave the tutorial waiting forever
- the tutorial no longer keeps naming an objective you've already done — after playing a card or picking one out of a selection, it could keep asking for a card that was by then sitting in your discard pile
- fixed the tutorial locking up after you play a card on slower machines — the tutorial could latch onto the card you just played, follow it into the discard pile, and then refuse to let you touch anything on the board
- fixed being stuck on PLAY in the tutorial battle intro — you can now arrow down to SKIP THIS BATTLE and SKIP ALL BATTLES
- fixed the tutorial battle intro never letting go: pressing PLAY left it holding the keyboard behind the match, and after the last tutorial battle it came back as an empty screen you couldn't escape from
